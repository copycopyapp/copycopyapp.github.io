A world clock for all people. See at a glance if it's a good time to reach the people you love, wherever they are.

  • Gold, coral and blue show who is awake
  • Find a time that works for everyone
  • Call or message them from their face on the clock

Quick answers

How do I delete a clock? Press and hold the clock and choose Delete World.

Where are the settings? Press and hold an empty spot on the board. With VoiceOver on, use the gear button at the top.

How do I change a place's hours? Tap its clock and use the From and Until wheels.

Does it work when I travel? Yes. My Time follows your iPhone's time zone.

More help is in the app: open settings and tap Help.

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026.

Light Here does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network connections.

Places and people you add stay on your device. When you add someone, you choose them in the iPhone's contact picker; Light Here keeps only that person's name, the number you picked and their picture, on your device. It never reads the rest of your contacts.

Calls and messages are started by the Phone, Messages, FaceTime, WhatsApp or Telegram app you choose, under that app's own privacy policy.

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.
