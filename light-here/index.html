Light Here

A world clock for all people. See at a glance if it's a good time to reach the people you love, wherever they are.

100% local No ads No accounts

What it does

Know at a glance

Gold means a good time, coral means early or late, and blue means they're probably asleep.

Find the right time

Slide ahead, or jump to the next time every place is awake, then share it.

Reach them

Call, message or FaceTime someone by tapping their face on the clock.

Display it anywhere

Made to stay on: your nightstand, your desk or an unused iPad. Night Look dims it after dark.

Change the look

Airport analog clocks, 70s LED or a split-flap board, plus widgets and StandBy.

Works offline

Over 159,000 cities and towns built in. It follows you when you travel.

How do I delete a clock?

Press and hold the clock and choose Delete World.

Where are the settings?

Press and hold an empty spot on the board. With VoiceOver on, use the gear button at the top. Settings also has Help.

How do I change a place's hours?

Tap its clock and use the From and Until wheels.

Does it follow me when I travel?

Yes. My Time follows your iPhone's time zone.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Data Storage

Light Here stores the places and people you add locally on your device. Nothing is ever transmitted to us or any third party.

No Data Collection

We do not collect, access, or receive any of your data. Light Here makes no network connections.

Contacts

When you add someone, you pick them in the iPhone's contact picker. Light Here keeps only that person's name, the number you chose and their picture, on your device. It never reads the rest of your contacts and needs no Contacts permission.

User-Initiated Calls and Messages

When you tap someone to reach them, the Phone, Messages, FaceTime, WhatsApp or Telegram app you choose opens directly. Light Here never logs this activity.

Widgets

Widgets read a copy of your clocks from storage shared only between the app and its widgets, on your device.

No Analytics or Tracking

Light Here contains no analytics, tracking, advertising, or crash reporting code.

Purchases

Light Here is purchased through the App Store. We do not receive or have access to your payment information.

Data Deletion

Delete any place or person in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all data.

Your Privacy, Simply

Nothing you add to Light Here is ever sent to us or anyone else. There are no servers, no accounts and no cloud.
